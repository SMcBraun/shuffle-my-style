Welcome to Shuffle My Style

Lots of clothes, but wearing the same few outfits? Mix what you already own into fresh new looks. No shopping needed.

  • Wear what you already own
  • Save money
  • Get dressed faster

How it works

  1. Fill your closet Add each piece: pick its bin and color.
  2. Shuffle Get 3 outfits made from your own clothes.
  3. Save and wear Keep your favorites and track what you wear.

Your closet saves in this browser, so it's ready when you come back.
